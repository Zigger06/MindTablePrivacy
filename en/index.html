Privacy questions

MindTable lets you play Chess, Russian Checkers, Go, and Nine Men’s Morris on one device or with a nearby opponent. This policy explains what information the app handles, what stays on your device, what is shared with other players and advertising services, and how you can manage your data.

01

The app and its developer

This policy covers MindTable, its Android version, local desktop versions, and this website. Zigger06 is the independent developer of MindTable. Developer profile: github.com/Zigger06. The privacy inquiry mechanism is provided in section 14.

MindTable does not create user accounts, require Google or social sign-in, or maintain its own server database of player profiles or matches. Advertising functions apply to Android builds in which advertising is enabled.

The game code does not request your contacts, messages, photos, microphone, camera, or precise GPS location. This does not mean that no data is processed: advertising and connection information are explained below.

02

Information on your device

MindTable stores information in the app’s storage area to provide the features you choose.

CategoryInformation and purpose
PreferencesLanguage, sound, music, vibration, animation, board and piece appearance, and Go board size. These keep your preferences between launches.
Unfinished local matchBoard position, move history, match state, and save time, to resume a game on one device. Network matches are not saved through this mechanism.

The developer does not automatically receive your preferences or local saves. Device or operating-system backup and restoration are governed by their own settings and terms.

03

Wi‑Fi and Bluetooth

Wi‑Fi play

During room discovery and a network match, devices exchange local IP addresses, ports, the host device model or label, a six-digit room code, a game identifier, connection messages, moves, and board state. MindTable sends discovery requests on the local network; the host responds with room information. This information can be accessed by other participants on that local network.

Bluetooth play

To connect, the game accesses already paired phones and computers, including their Bluetooth names and addresses, and uses them to find a room. The room code, connection messages, and game actions are exchanged directly between connected devices. The game code does not upload the paired-device list to a developer server or AdMob.

The developer does not receive the move stream for these matches. Your opponent receives game state and may independently keep a copy, for example by taking a screenshot. The advertising SDK may separately access the internet while you use local game features.

04

Android permissions

  • Internet and network state: advertising, consent checks, and network connections.
  • Wi‑Fi state and multicast discovery: finding game rooms on the local network.
  • Bluetooth / Nearby devices: connecting to already paired devices. On applicable Android versions, the app requests the Bluetooth Connect permission.

System labels vary by Android version. The game does not use Bluetooth scanning to determine location or request precise location permission. Denying Bluetooth permission disables that connection option but does not prevent play on one device. You can change permissions in Android settings.

05

Advertising and Google services

The Android version uses Google AdMob / Google Mobile Ads SDK and Google User Messaging Platform (UMP). Ads may include a home-screen banner and occasional displays after completed matches; the game limits their frequency.

The advertising SDK may collect and share with Google and parties involved in advertising:

  • IP address and approximate region inferred from it;
  • app and ad interactions, such as launches, taps, and views;
  • technical information about app, SDK, and device performance;
  • Android advertising ID, App Set ID, and other available identifiers.

Purposes include serving and measuring ads, technical analysis, and fraud prevention. Processing varies with device settings, identifier availability, region, and consent choices. Non-personalized or limited ads do not necessarily mean that no technical information is processed.

The developer may access AdMob reports about impressions, ad performance, and revenue. MindTable does not use a separate developer-operated behavioral analytics service. See Google’s Privacy Policy, Google advertising technologies, and Mobile Ads SDK data disclosure.

06

Purposes and legal bases

The main purposes are to provide the game functions you request, remember local preferences, connect you with an opponent, fund the app through advertising, respect advertising choices, and respond to inquiries.

Where applicable law requires a legal basis, processing for requested functions is necessary to provide those functions; processing that requires consent relies on your choice; security and responding to inquiries may rely on legitimate interests balanced against your rights; mandatory processing relies on legal obligations. Google and other providers explain the bases for their own processing in their policies.

Reading this policy or using the game does not itself replace separate consent for processing that requires it.

07

Who receives information

  • Nearby devices and your opponent: room-discovery and match information needed to connect and play.
  • Google and advertising providers: advertising and consent information according to configuration and available UMP forms. Where a form is available, information about participating providers can be found in it.
  • GitHub: hosting this website and handling inquiries you submit through GitHub.
  • Competent authorities: information held by the developer, only where disclosure is required by applicable law.

The game code does not send local saves, Bluetooth pairing lists, or moves to a developer server to sell them or build advertising profiles. Certain advertising data transfers may be considered “sharing” under some regional laws; applicable law and advertising settings determine available opt-out choices.

08

How long information is kept

  • Local preferences and ad counters remain until deleted, app storage is cleared, or the app is uninstalled; system backups may be managed separately.
  • Unfinished local matches remain until replaced by a new save, removed by the game, for example when a match finishes, or app storage is cleared.
  • Discovery and connection information is used in memory during room discovery and a session. Room lists are cleared when discovery stops, and connections close on disconnection. Android itself retains the system’s Bluetooth pairing list.
  • Google data is retained under the relevant service’s rules, considering purposes, settings, and legal duties. The developer does not set a universal retention period for Google’s servers. See Google’s retention practices.
  • Inquiries are used as needed to respond, resolve the request, and meet mandatory obligations. Public GitHub messages may remain under the platform’s rules; closing an issue does not automatically delete it.
09

Deleting your information

There is no MindTable account to delete. To remove local information:

  1. Reset settings in the game to restore default game preferences. This is not a deletion of all saved matches or SDK data.
  2. To remove all app data on Android, open Settings → Apps → MindTable → Storage → Clear data / Clear storage. Labels may differ. This removes saves, preferences, and SDK data from the app’s local storage.
  3. Uninstalling also removes local app data according to Android’s rules. Check system backups if you want to prevent later restoration.

On a computer, you can remove MindTable’s user-data directory using operating-system tools. Clearing the app does not delete data already sent to Google or GitHub or copies kept by an opponent. Use the relevant service’s controls and contact channels for those records. If you provided information to the developer in an inquiry, you can request deletion through the contact mechanism below.

10

Your choices and rights

Where your region or advertising configuration requires consent, UMP displays the appropriate form. Use the available advertising privacy button in MindTable settings to revise your choice; it appears when UMP indicates that an entry point is required.

You can manage your advertising ID in Android settings, deny Bluetooth permission, and end network play. Withdrawal affects future processing and does not by itself invalidate lawful processing already completed.

Depending on applicable law, you may have rights of access, correction, deletion, restriction, portability, objection, consent withdrawal, and complaint to a data-protection authority. Contact the developer about information the developer holds; for information independently processed by Google or GitHub, also use their privacy channels. A response may require the minimum information needed to locate your inquiry and verify authority, without excessive documentation.

11

Younger players

MindTable is a board-game app. It has no registration, child profile, or date-of-birth request. The developer does not ask children for a name, phone number, address, or similar information to play.

Parents and guardians should review device permissions and advertising controls. If you believe a child has provided personal information to the developer, use the contact mechanism below without publishing that information.

Not asking for age does not prevent technical information from being handled by the advertising SDK. Available advertising and consent options depend on applicable requirements, build settings, and advertising services.

12

Security and processing locations

Local files are kept in the app storage area protected by operating-system controls. Google states that Mobile Ads SDK data is encrypted in transit using TLS. Google and GitHub may process information in countries other than yours; their policies explain applicable safeguards and international-transfer mechanisms.

Use trusted networks and devices for local play. A six-digit code identifies a room; it is not a secret password. Wi‑Fi discovery and game information do not have separate MindTable end-to-end encryption. Bluetooth uses the operating system’s pairing and security mechanisms.

No storage or transmission method provides absolute security. The developer does not ask for passwords, payment information, or identity documents through the game. Do not provide them in public inquiries.

13

This website and external links

This website consists of static pages. Its code contains no advertising SDKs, analytics, tracking pixels, data-collection forms, cookies, or localStorage writes. Changing language opens an ordinary page; printing is handled by your browser.

The website is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may handle request information, such as IP address, browser information, and access time, to provide and secure its services. See GitHub’s Privacy Statement.

Following links to Google, GitHub, or Google Play brings you under the selected service’s policies. Information you voluntarily submit through GitHub Issues, including your account name and message, is handled by GitHub and may be publicly visible.
